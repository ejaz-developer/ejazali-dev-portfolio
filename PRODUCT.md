# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and prospective freelance clients evaluating Ejaz Ali's ability to build and ship software. They visit to understand his technical range, inspect real projects, and decide whether to start a professional conversation or consider him for work.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Ejaz Ali's full-stack development work, experience, services, and contact options in one place. Its primary success measure is building credibility through clear evidence of shipped projects and making it easy for qualified visitors to continue the conversation.

## Positioning

The portfolio demonstrates end-to-end software work across interfaces, APIs, databases, and production deployments, rather than presenting only isolated tools or skills.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the landing page sections for selected work, background, services, experience, and contact information. They can inspect project previews and source repositories, then use email, phone, or social links to follow up. The site also provides privacy and terms pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current product is a Next.js web application using the App Router.
- The main route presents Hero, About, Projects, Services, Experience, and Contact sections.
- Project records include descriptions, technology tags, source repositories, and live previews where available.
- Existing privacy and terms routes must remain available.
- Preserve the current identity, contact details, social profiles, and project facts unless the user explicitly changes them.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, performance metrics, project outcomes, or other evidence.
- Future interaction and animation work must respect reduced-motion preferences and remain usable by keyboard and assistive technology.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ejaz Ali
- Role: Full-stack developer
- Voice: Direct, practical, and grounded in software that ships and holds up under real use.
- Preserve the existing personal portrait, project imagery, contact details, GitHub profile, X profile, and email links.

## Evidence on Hand

- Personal portrait: `public/my-hero-image.avif`
- Project previews: `public/project-images/AudioThought.avif`, `public/project-images/RecordKeeper-.avif`, and `public/project-images/CogniClick.avif`
- Project and experience data: `data/data.ts`
- Source and live project links are recorded in `data/data.ts`.
- No testimonials, client case studies, or quantified project outcomes are present; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Show shipped work before making broad claims.
- Make technical breadth understandable through concrete project evidence.
- Keep the path from evaluation to contact short and clear.
- Preserve factual accuracy over persuasive embellishment.
- Treat accessibility and reduced motion as product requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, readable contrast, semantic structure, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support are required for future work. The portfolio should remain usable across desktop and mobile web contexts.
