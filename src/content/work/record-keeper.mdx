## The Problem

Small businesses track sales and ledgers in notebooks or spreadsheets: entries get lost, totals are added by hand, and nobody has a trusted view of what was sold or owed at the end of the day.

## The Build

RecordKeeper is a point-of-sale system built with Next.js and Appwrite. It records sales, maintains customer ledgers, and gives a business owner a single place to see what was sold, what's outstanding, and who owes what — replacing the paper-and-spreadsheet process.

## Challenges

Modeling ledger state so a payment, partial payment, and refund all roll up correctly without requiring the owner to do manual reconciliation.

## Effect

Effects will be added once the business using it has recorded enough sales to measure time saved and errors removed.
