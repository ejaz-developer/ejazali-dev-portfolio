## The Problem

Thoughts show up as voice memos, rambling notes, and half-finished drafts. Capturing them is easy; turning them into something structured and manageable — a mindmap, an outline, a plan — is the manual part that gets skipped.

## The Build

AudioThought transcribes voice input with Assembly AI, stores transcripts and sessions in Supabase, and presents them through a Next.js frontend so a spoken thought can be reviewed, edited, and shaped into structure instead of living as an unsearchable recording.

## Challenges

Keeping the transcription-to-structure flow fast enough that speaking feels like the path of least resistance, and deciding how much structure to impose automatically versus leave for the user to shape.

Not yet measured — the project is still in development. Effects will be added once it ships.
